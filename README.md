# Illustration Portfolio — Braeden Baerlocher

A selection of my original artwork, including pencil creature and character designs and a self-portrait created in Adobe Photoshop.

My pencil work uses expressive lines to explore shape and personality. The visible sketch lines are an intentional part of my style. The Photoshop self-portrait shows my digital image-making work.

I’m a Media Arts graduate with experience in drawing, digital design, video editing, and game development.
